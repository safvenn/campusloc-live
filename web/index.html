Connect a device

  1. Connect the phone to your laptop hotspot or the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. Download the Android APK on the phone and install it.
  3. Enter a display name and the broker address below in CampusLoc.
  4. Enable Wi-Fi and Location, grant precise location permission, and tap Start tracking. Keep the app open.

If this page is opened as localhost, replace it with your laptop’s hotspot/LAN IP. A hotspot does not identify floors by itself.
